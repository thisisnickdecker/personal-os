**How it should flow:** the First Hour is the low-priced front door, and it converts into Dialed In or Second Brain, then into On Call.

**Year-1 model (part-time, about 15 hrs/week)**
| | Conservative | Target |
|---|---|---|
| First Hours | 40 | 80 |
| Dialed In / Second Brain / Money Map (avg ~$850) | 15 | 35 |
| Builds (avg ~$2,000) | 2 | 6 |
| On Call members by year end | 10 | 25 |
| **Year-1 revenue** | **about $25k** | **about $60k** |
| Recurring at year end | about $1k/mo | about $2.5k/mo |

**Ways to grow past solo hours:**
- **Group workshops:** "AI for Your Household" at $49–99 a seat, 10–20 people, through libraries, churches, schools and senior centers. These are the best lead source.
- **A self-serve "Household AI Starter Kit"** (guide + prompts + templates) at $29–49.
- **Train 1–2 "household AI guides"** and keep 30–40% of what they bill.
- **Small businesses:** the same menu at about 2× price.

**Realistic ceiling:** $150–250k/yr as a small practice. Bigger only with workshops, a product or guides.

**Costs:**
- Domain about $11–80/yr
- Booking/payment tool (Cal.com/Stripe) $0–15/mo
- Your own AI subscriptions about $40–120/mo
- E&O insurance about $65–75/mo once you're doing paid work

## Tool menu (late 2026, verify prices before quoting)
| Tool | Price (per month) | Best for | Watch out |
|---|---|---|---|
| ChatGPT | Free; Go $8; Plus $20; Pro $100–200 | All-rounder, voice, images | Ads on Free/Go |
| Claude | Free; Pro $20; Max from $100 ([pricing](https://claude.com/pricing)) | Writing, long docs, files, Projects, connectors, Cowork desktop agent | No image generation; agent work uses up Pro limits |
| Claude Code | Included with Pro/Max | Building your own apps by describing them | Max tier for heavy use |
| Gemini | Free; AI Pro $19.99 (incl. 5 TB storage) | Google-centric families (Gmail/Calendar/Android) | Weaker outside Google |
| Grok | Free; SuperGrok $30 | Real-time news/X, voice | Few integrations; brand fit for families |
| Perplexity (+ Comet browser) | Free; Pro $20 | Cited research; browser that does tasks | Weak for long writing |
| Parallel ([parallel.ai](https://parallel.ai/pricing)) | API, pay per request | Web search and research engine *inside* custom helpers (developer tool, not a consumer app) | Not something clients use directly |
| Obsidian / Notion | Obsidian free (+Sync $4–5); Notion AI in the Business tier ($20–24) | Second brain | Obsidian has a learning curve; Notion AI is gated |

Only Claude's prices were confirmed on the vendor's own page; the rest come from third-party trackers.

**Default stacks to recommend:**
- **Non-technical household, about $20/mo:** one main assistant. Gemini AI Pro if the family lives in Google; ChatGPT Plus or Claude Pro otherwise. Add Perplexity Free.
- **Solo pro / side hustle, about $40/mo:** Claude Pro (writing, Projects per client, connectors) plus ChatGPT Plus or Gemini.
- **Tinkerer, about $100–130/mo:** Claude Max (Claude Code) + Obsidian vault + the Parallel API for custom helpers.
