# The Familiar

An always-on AI box for the home: I set households up with a Mac mini running useful AI helpers (the Doorman text force field, a daily brief, money organization, a second brain, custom agents), then stay on as their AI IT person and coach.

## Why now
- In 2026 people rushed to buy Mac minis to run always-on agents (OpenClaw), and done-for-you setup already sells for $997–2,600. Almost all of it is for businesses or technical early adopters, done once.
- The open lane: **households, in person, with ongoing care and coaching** ("good householder" AI).
- Dario Amodei: capability is arriving fast. Adoption, trust and people are the bottleneck. Setup will get commoditized, while coaching, safety and trust last.

## The offer (prices to test)
- **Move-in:** Remote $750, or Home (Cincinnati / New Richmond) $1,250. Client buys the Mac mini directly; no hardware markup (Apple sells to end users only).
- **Jobs menu:**
  - The Doorman ("practice safe text") $250
  - Daily Brief $250
  - Money Map $500
  - Second Brain $500
  - Voice Agent $750+
  - Custom Agent $1,500+
- **Householder coaching:** $150/hr, or 4 sessions for $500
- **Stewardship care plan:** Watch $49/mo, Steward $149/mo, Concierge $399/mo

## Cloud or box
Hybrid. The box handles iMessage, the Doorman and private local data; a client's Apple ID on a cloud Mac risks an account lock. Everything else can run in the cloud. A cheaper "Familiar Cloud" tier serves clients who want no hardware.

## Risks
- Agent security: 40k+ exposed OpenClaw instances, plus prompt injection
- Liability around finances
- Apple changing iMessage access
- Commoditization
- Home visits eating the week

Guardrails:
- Client owns every account, and I never hold their passwords
- Agents read and summarize; a human taps before money moves or messages are sent
- E&O + cyber insurance

## 30-day test
1. Be customer zero: run my own Familiar and write the runbook.
2. Have 15 conversations.
3. Set up 3 founding households at $500 with 3 months of Steward free.
4. Decision gate: 3 paid setups and at least 1 paid Steward plan means build the site and film the demo. Otherwise fold it into the Opportunity Bureau's offers.
