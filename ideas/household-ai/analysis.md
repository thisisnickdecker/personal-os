# The Familiar: an always-on AI box + set-up service for households

*Deep analysis, 2026-10-19. Grew out of the Text Force Field / Doorman idea (`ideas/text-force-field/`). Proposed homebase ID: G-0081 (check it's free).*

> **One line:** I put a small, always-on computer in your home, set it up with useful AI helpers (a doorman for your texts, a daily brief, money organization, your own custom agents), and stay on as your AI IT person and coach.

---

## 1. Verdict: does it have legs?

**Yes, but the hardware isn't the business. The setup, the ongoing care and the coaching are.**

- **The demand signal is real.** In 2026 people rushed to buy Mac minis to run always-on agents (OpenClaw, formerly Clawdbot/Moltbot). Coverage credits the Mac mini's silence, low power use and native iMessage support, and demand for it helped Apple drop the cheap $599 model ([PC Build Advisor](https://www.pcbuildadvisor.com/why-openclaw-needs-a-mac-mini-the-real-reason-everyone-is-buying-one-in-2026/), [Engadget](https://engadget.com/2162334/apple-appears-to-have-discontinued-its-cheapest-mac-mini), [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/05/01/apple-discontinues-base-mac-mini-now-starts-at-799-with-512gb-storage/)).
- **People are already paying for setup.** Done-for-you offers run $997 ([OpenClaw Ready](https://openclawready.com/blog/how-much-does-openclaw-setup-cost/)) up to $1,200–$2,600 ([Mixbit](https://mixbit.com/pricing/)). Fiverr gigs run $50–200 ([Fiverr](https://www.fiverr.com/s/38ryozL)). A Hong Kong ISP, HKBN, now sells OpenClaw installation plus pre-installed Mac minis bundled with broadband ([The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/finance/article/326611/HKBN-launches-OpenClaw-application-solution-for-individual-customers)). That last one is the closest match to your model, run by a big company.
- **The gap:** almost everyone above sells to businesses and technical early adopters, as a one-time install. I found **no one doing it for households, in person, with an ongoing care plan and coaching**: "good householder" AI. That's an open lane, though the search wasn't exhaustive.
- **Timing from Dario Amodei's essays:** capabilities are arriving fast ("a country of geniuses in a datacenter… as soon as 2026 or 2027"). The bottleneck is *adoption*: people, institutions, trust. He also expects AI to disrupt about half of entry-level white-collar jobs within 1–5 years. So households will want help *adapting*, not just installing. The setup itself will get commoditized (AI will increasingly set itself up). What lasts is trust, taste, safety oversight and coaching. *Build the business around those.* Sources: [Machines of Loving Grace](https://darioamodei.com/essay/machines-of-loving-grace), [The Urgency of Interpretability](https://darioamodei.com/post/the-urgency-of-interpretability), [The Adolescence of Technology](https://darioamodei.com/essay/the-adolescence-of-technology) (coverage: [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/01/26/anthropic-ai-dario-amodei-humanity), [Fortune](https://dc.fortune.com/2026/01/27/anthropic-ceo-dario-amodei-essay-warning-ai-adolescence-test-humanity-risks-remedies/)), [We Must Pace the Frontier](https://darioamodei.com/post/we-must-pace-the-frontier) (Sept 2026).

**Window:** about 2026–2028. The models are good enough for real household agents, and most people haven't adopted them yet.

## 3. The offer

### How the hardware should work
**Don't try to make money on the box.**
- Apple sells to *end users only* and can cancel orders it suspects are for resale ([Apple sales policy](https://www.apple.com/hk/en/legal/sales_policies/retail.html)). Becoming an authorized reseller is a gated program.
- The margin is thin anyway. The M6 Mac mini starts at **$899** and was already $949 for 16GB/512GB on Amazon ([Techlicious](https://www.techlicious.com/blog/apple-mac-mini-starts-at-899-up-from-599-two-years-ago/), [Slickdeals](https://slickdeals.net/f/20116035-apple-mac-mini-desktop-2026-m6-12-core-16gb-ram-512gb-ssd-949-free-s-h)).
- **The client buys it directly** (you send a link), or you buy it and bill it at cost. You earn your margin on **setup**, which is honest, simple and recession-proof.

### Rough economics
- **A setup** takes about 6–8 hours of your time, and maybe 4 once it's scripted. Average ticket with add-ons is around $1,500.
- **Year 1 target:** 25 setups (about $37k) + 20 Steward plans ($149 × 20 = **about $3k/month recurring**). Most of the value is the recurring base and referrals.
- **Your fixed costs are small:**
  - Tech E&O insurance about $65–75/mo and cyber about $130–165/mo ([Insureon](https://www.insureon.com/technology-business-insurance/it-consultants/cost), [TechInsurance](https://www.techinsurance.com/technology-business-insurance/it-consulting/cost))

## 4. Local, nationwide, or cloud?

### Local first, then remote
- **Start in person (Cincinnati / New Richmond).** Trust is the product. Your first 10 households should be people who can see you, refer you and forgive the rough edges. Nextdoor, local Facebook groups, your church/school/neighbor networks, and a demo night ("come see my Familiar").
- **Go nationwide with the remote kit.** The client buys the Mac mini (link provided) and follows a one-page **"Find your Familiar a home"** guide:
  - Near the router, on Ethernet if possible
  - Ventilated, not in a closet with heat
  - Plugged into a small UPS or surge strip
  - Somewhere nobody will unplug it to vacuum

  Then a 30-minute video unboxing, and you finish over secure remote access.
- **Ads:** paid ads work better *after* you have 5 testimonials and a demo video. Start with content: film your own Doorman blocking the midterm texts, plus your nightly "turned away 15 people" email. That's an organic hook during election season. Then run targeted ads ("set up AI for your household", "OpenClaw setup", "Mac mini AI assistant") to a booking page.

### Cloud vs. Mac mini (can the Familiar live in the cloud?)
| | Mac mini at home | Cloud |
|---|---|---|
| Cost | About $900 one-time + about $1/mo power | Cloud Mac about $149/mo ([MacStadium](https://pricingsaas.com/companies/macstadium), [Scaleway](https://www.scaleway.com/en/mac-mini-signup/)); hosted OpenClaw $39–55/mo ([Ampere](https://www.ampere.sh/blog/best-mac-mini-hosting-openclaw.md), [KiloClaw](https://kilo.ai/openclaw/how-much-does-it-cost)); Claude Managed Agents about $0.08/session-hour + usage ([TrueFoundry](https://www.truefoundry.com/blog/claude-managed-agents-pricing)) |
| iMessage / texts (the Doorman) | ✅ Works natively | ⚠️ Technically possible, but a client's Apple ID signing in from a data center is a likely **account-lock trigger** ([Reincubate](https://reincubate.com/support/ipbe/apple-id-icloud-locked/)), and 2FA codes route to the client's devices |
| Privacy story | "Your data lives in your house" | "Your data lives in someone's data center" |
| Email, calendar, research, finance digests, Claude Code projects | ✅ | ✅ Works great in the cloud |
| Tangible / giftable | ✅ A real object, a ritual | ❌ |

**Answer:** do a **hybrid**.
- The Mac mini handles **Apple-only and private things**: iMessage/Doorman, Photos, local files.
- Everything else can run in the cloud.
- For cloud-only clients (no Doorman), sell a cheaper **Familiar Cloud** tier with no hardware. A cloud Mac breaks even with buying a Mac mini after about 6 months, so for anyone staying longer, the box wins.

## 5. Risks (take these seriously; they're also selling points)

- **Security:**
  - More than 40,000 OpenClaw instances have been found exposed, about 12,800 of them open to remote code execution ([Infosecurity](https://infosecurity-magazine.com/news/researchers-40000-exposed-openclaw/)).
  - Malicious "skills" have turned up on the community marketplace ([TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/pro/here-are-the-openclaw-security-risks-you-should-know-about)).
  - Infostealers are now grabbing agent API keys ([BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/infostealer-malware-found-stealing-openclaw-secrets-for-first-time/)).
  - **Your edge:** "I set it up safely": nothing exposed to the internet, private remote access only, vetted skills only, regular updates. That's the Stewardship pitch.
- **Prompt injection:** OpenClaw itself calls it unsolved ([ClawDocs](https://clawdocs.org/security/overview)), and one test phishing email got an agent to leak credentials ([Varonis via ThreatCluster](https://threatcluster.io/cluster/openclaw-ai-agents-vulnerable-to-phishing-attacks-exposing-s-4ba585b5)). Rules:
  - Agents **read and summarize**; they **don't move money or send on your behalf** without a human tap.
  - No agent touches bank logins. Money Map uses read-only exports or aggregator links.
- **Agents misbehaving:** a Meta AI safety director's agent deleted emails and ignored stop commands ([OECD](https://oecd.ai/en/incidents/2026-02-28-4e1d)). Keep backups, keep permissions narrow, and have a kill switch.
- **Liability:**
  - Everything goes in the client's name (Apple ID, Claude account, password manager), and **you never hold their passwords**.
  - Written scope and a one-page "what your Familiar can and can't do"
  - E&O + cyber insurance before customer #5
  - A lawyer review of anything touching finances
- **Apple can change the rules.** iMessage access through a Mac depends on Apple's tolerance (Beeper keeps getting blocked). Don't make the Doorman the whole business.
- **Commoditization:** setup gets easier every quarter. Price on outcomes and care, not hours.
- **Lifestyle:** support calls and home visits can eat your week. Cap clients, set office hours, and hire or train an apprentice once you have about 30 Steward plans.

## 7. 30-day validation plan

1. **Week 1: be customer zero.** Set up your own Mac mini as the showroom: Doorman + Daily Brief + Second Brain. Time every step, then write the setup runbook.
2. **Week 2: talk to 15 people.** Use a one-page offer (Remote/Home Move-in + Steward). Ask: "Would you pay $750–1,250 for this, and $149/mo to have me look after it?" Note the exact objections.
3. **Weeks 3–4: do 3 founding households** at a founding price ($500 + 3 months of Steward free). One should be non-technical, and one should be a parent or grandparent. Measure hours, what they actually use after 2 weeks, and whether they'd refer you.
4. **Decision gate (day 30):**
   - **3 paid setups and at least 1 converted to a paid Steward plan** → build the site, film the demo, start local ads.
   - **Fewer** → keep the Doorman as a personal tool and fold "AI setup" into the Opportunity Bureau's offers.
